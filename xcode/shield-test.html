What this page cannot prove

Two pieces of the Shield leave no mark a page can show. The cookie and Referer dressing — cross-site subresources carrying no cookies, and only the asking page's origin as a Referer — can only be seen by a service that echoes back what it received; the README describes how to look. And the lists themselves are curated starters: a tracker or a den on nobody's list yet will still load, and the README says that plainly too.
