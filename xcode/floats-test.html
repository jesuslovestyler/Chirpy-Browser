Chirpy Engine 0.17 — floats test

Every box on this page is drawn with CSS alone. Read down and check each numbered promise.

1 — a box floated left inside a paragraph's flow

FLOATED BOX — the text should wrap around my right side, then resume full width below me.

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog, and the paragraph keeps flowing beside the blue box for as many lines as the box is tall. Pack my box with five dozen liquor jugs: the words should stay clear of the box the whole way down, hugging its right edge, and only when the text passes the box's bottom should the lines stretch out to the full width of the column again. If any word hides underneath the blue, the float has failed; if the lines stay narrow all the way to the end of the paragraph, the wrap never let go. Either way you will see it at a glance, which is the point of a test page.

2 — a box floated right

FLOATED RIGHT — the text should wrap around my left side.

Same promise, other side: this paragraph should run down the left of the red box, its lines ending before the box begins, and once the text is past the box's bottom the lines should widen to the full column again. A sidebar, an infobox, a portrait photograph — half the pages on the web are this one shape repeated.

3 — two floated columns in a colored wrapper

LEFT COLUMN — I float left at 44% of the page. The yellow wrapper behind us should stretch down to contain us both, even though we are its only children.
RIGHT COLUMN — I float right at 44%. Between us there should be a gutter of yellow, and the paragraph after the wrapper should begin below the yellow, not beside us.

This paragraph follows the wrapper. It must start below the yellow box, at full width — proof the wrapper contained its floats.
