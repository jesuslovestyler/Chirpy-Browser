Promise 3 — fixed stays

The green bar at the top of the window is position:fixed, set with top, left and right and no width at all — its width is derived from the two insets against the window itself. It is a real overlay view, reseated over the page on every scroll. Everything in this section is filler so the page is tall enough to prove it: keep scrolling and watch the bar refuse to move.

Filler, so the page stands tall. A fixed box is measured against the viewport — the part of the page you can see — and not against the document, which is why it can hold its place while the document moves under it. Browsers have done this since the earliest style sheets: a fixed bar is the page's furniture, bolted to the window frame.

More filler. The bar above should still read “FIXED BAR” in white on green, spanning the whole width of the window, its right edge flush with the window's right edge. If it has drifted, narrowed, or scrolled even a little, promise 3 is broken and its number names it.
