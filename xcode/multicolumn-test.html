Chirpy Engine 0.22 — multi-column & color-mix test

Every effect on this page is drawn with CSS alone. Read down and check each numbered promise. One honest note before the promises: the words inside a column block are paint, not live text — the floated boxes' bargain since 0.17. You cannot select them, and a link inside a column block shows its colors but does not click.

2 — columns by width, not by count

This block declares a column WIDTH (220 points) and no count at all. The engine fits as many columns of about that width as the page holds — four stand here — each the same width as its neighbours, with a gap of 24 between them and a thin dotted blue rule standing in each gap. The words flow down the first column and continue at the top of the second, exactly as they did in section 1.

Following text — below the block, full width, as always.

Second paragraph: it follows the first through the columns, filling what the first one left. However the words divide, the two columns should stand about even with each other, and nothing here should spill into a third column — the block was promised two and two it keeps.

The wrapper here paints its own green behind three columns of white words. The green should stand behind ALL the columns as one unbroken surface — behind the gaps too — not as three green stripes with dark seams between them. The words themselves divide into three columns inside the color, reading down one and continuing at the top of the next, the same promise as section 1 wearing a coat.

Following text — below the green, full width.

5 — color-mix swatches

A — color-mix(in srgb, red 50%, blue 50%): a true purple, halfway between red and blue.
B — color-mix(in srgb, red 25%, blue 75%): the same pair, weighted — a blue-violet, much nearer blue.
C — color-mix(in srgb, white, black), no percentages written: the spec's default is half and half, so a middle gray.
D — color-mix(in srgb, red, yellow): orange — the mix red and yellow make — carrying dark text on top of it.
E — a mix inside a mix: color-mix(in srgb, color-mix(in srgb, red, blue), white) — the purple of swatch A lightened halfway toward white, a pale lavender.
F — a gradient whose two stops are mixes: orange (red + yellow) at the top melting into a teal green-blue (blue + green) at the bottom.
G — the mix arrived by variable: --brand was defined on :root as color-mix(in srgb, navy 70%, white), and this band's background is var(--brand) — a muted, lightened navy.

H — this heading's TEXT is a mix: black 65% into white, a dark gray rather than true black.

6 — the honest limit: column-span is not read yet

A heading asking to span all columns

The heading above declares column-span: all, which in a full browser would stretch it across both columns. Chirpy 0.22 does not read column-span yet, so the truthful thing to check here is the opposite: the heading stays INSIDE one column like everything else in the block, and the text flows around it within the columns. When span is someday read, this promise gets rewritten.

End of the test page. If the columns stood, balanced, and ruled, and the swatches wore the colors their labels promised, multi-column and color-mix work.
