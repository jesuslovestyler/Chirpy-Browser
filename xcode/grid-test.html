Chirpy Engine 0.30 — grid test

Every arrangement on this page is made with CSS grid alone. Read down and check each numbered promise. First, the status line at the bottom of the window should read "Chirpy Engine 0.30.0 (Swift)". Honest notes before the promises: the items on this page are paint, not live text — the floated boxes' bargain since 0.17 — so a link inside an item shows its colors but does not click; and baseline alignment of items still reads as start. Since 0.30 the rest of the old outs are read too: grid-auto-flow: column and dense packing, named lines and grid-template-areas — promises 9 through 12 below — and a container's percentage height, promise 13.

LEFT — my column is 1fr: one share of the width the two gutters leave.
MIDDLE — my column is 2fr, so I stand twice as wide as each side card: two shares against their one.
RIGHT — 1fr again. The yellow gutters between us are 20 points each, and there is no gutter at the row's edges.

9 — auto-flow: column fills downward

DOWN 1 — first cell of the first column.
DOWN 2 — below me: the flow runs down the column, not across the row.
DOWN 3 — the two declared rows are full, so I open the second column and stand at its top, beside DOWN 1.
DOWN 4 — second column, second row, beside DOWN 2.

Read this grid down, then across: that is grid-auto-flow: column, the rows' own flow turned on its side.

10 — dense packing backfills

WIDE 1 — I span two of the three columns, leaving one cell at the first row's end.
WIDE 2 — I span two as well, so I cannot fit in that one cell: I open the second row.
SMALL — I span one. Under plain flow I would stand after WIDE 2 in the second row; under dense packing the cursor goes back and I fill the hole in the FIRST row instead.

If the small green box stands at the first row's right end, dense packing works.

11 — line names

BY NAME, WIDE — my grid-column is middle / end: I stand in the wide fr track, because the template named its lines [start], [middle] and [end].
BY NAME, NARROW — my grid-column is start / middle: the fixed 200 track. I am written second but stand first, because my named lines put me there.

12 — template areas

HEAD — grid-area: head puts me across the top, both columns wide.
SIDE — the narrow column of the middle row.
MAIN — the wide column of the middle row, beside SIDE.
FOOT — across the bottom, both columns wide, under SIDE and MAIN alike.

The template drew a map — "head head", "side main", "foot foot" — and each box went to the rectangle its name fills.

13 — a percentage height against a definite one

HALF — this grid is height: 50% inside the pale step around it, which declares 300: the grid stands half the step's inside height, its row stretched to fill it, and the pale ground shows the other half below.

And where no ancestor declares a height, a grid container's percentage height still reads as its content's own height — as every grid above this one stands.

End of the test page. If all thirteen promises held, grid works.
