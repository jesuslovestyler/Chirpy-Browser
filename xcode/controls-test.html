Chirpy Engine 0.31 — live select & textarea controls test

Read down and check each numbered promise — every one of them is worked by hand: a popup only proves itself opened, and an editor only proves itself typed in. First, the status line at the bottom of the window should read "Chirpy Engine 0.31.0 (Swift)". Honest notes before the promises: a popup wears the page's font and, when one is declared, its background as a tint — its rows' text and the popup's own chrome are the system's and take no CSS dress, and neither do a CSS border or corner radius on it; picking several options at once in a multiple select is not built (7 shows what stands instead); a placeholder clears at the first focus and does not come back, and it is never the field's value; Enter in an editor makes a new line, never a submission; choosing in a popup submits nothing on its own; and a control inside a floated box keeps the standing bracket-label dress, as 6 shows, so the live forms stand in ordinary flow.

1 — a popup, its groups and its resting choice

Promise: the control is a real popup button, resting on "Fried fish". Open it: the five dishes stand in order under two dimmed group rows, "Mains" and "Sweets", which cannot themselves be chosen. Choose any dish — the popup's face takes its label, and the choice is yours to change.

2 — a seeded editor


Promise: a real editor, four rows tall, already holding the page's two lines. Click into it and type — your words join the page's, the box scrolls as the text outgrows it, and Enter makes a new line rather than sending anything.

3 — the Send press

Promise: press Send (after choosing a dish in 1 and adding words in 2, if you like) and the window sets off for this page's own address with the gathered values riding on it — watch the address bar: ?dish=…&words=… — the dish's value (fish, not "Fried fish") and your editor's current words, percent-encoded as a browser sends them.

4 — a popup in the page's dress

Promise: the popup wears the page's dress as far as a native popup honestly can — Georgia at 17 points, its gold background standing as the tint — resting on "Sweet tea". Its menu's rows keep the system's own dress, as the notes at the top say.

5 — a styled editor and its placeholder


Promise: an editor in the page's dress — dark ground, cream words, a gold border — holding its placeholder in gray because the page seeded it empty. Click into it: the placeholder clears and stays gone, your words land in cream, and while the focus lasts the declared outline stands as an amber ring around the box.

Its own Send carries the dressed values the same way — ?drink=tea and your note — by the same road as 3.

6 — the same select, floated

Supper in the floated box:

Promise: inside a painted box the popup cannot live, so it keeps the standing stand-in — a bracketed label wearing its resting choice: [Sweet tea].

The box floats; its select stands as paint, as every control in a painted box has since the floats came.

7 — a multiple select

Promise: it seats as an ordinary popup, resting on "Collard greens", its first chosen option, and opening it chooses one, as any popup does. Picking several at once — the listbox a multiple select draws in other browsers — is not built, and is named here rather than faked.
