Chirpy Engine 0.26 — the interaction set test

Every effect on this page is worked with CSS alone. Read down and check each numbered promise. First, the status line at the bottom of the window should read "Chirpy Engine 0.26.0 (Swift)". Honest notes before the promises: selects and textareas are live controls since 0.31, with a page of their own (controls-test.html) — none stands here, so none is dressed or promised here; a checkbox is a glyph, not a live control, and takes its element's color only; a native button wears its background as its tint and its color as its label's, and a button's CSS border and corner radius have no native dress to ride on, so none is promised for it; focus on a link is given by a press, as in a real browser — the Tab key does not walk this page's links; a control seated inside a floated, flexed, gridded or columned box keeps the standing bracket-label dress, so the form below stands in ordinary flow; a sticky element inside such a painted box flows plain, so both sticky bars below stand in ordinary flow too; and sticking is read for the vertical scroll only — top and bottom, never left or right.

1 — a form dressed by the page

The form below declares its own colors, and its live controls wear them: the fields stand dark slate with gold borders and rounded corners, their letters cream in Georgia at 16px — not the uniform system dress every field wore before 0.26. The checkbox glyph stands gold, the color its input declares. The submit stands dark gold with a white label.



The glyph beside this line is gold because its input says so.

Now the focus, which needs your hands: click into either field. While the focus rests there the field wears its :focus dress — a lighter slate ground, a brighter gold border, and the orange outline the page declares rides as its ring. Click back out and the resting dress returns. And press-and-hold the Subscribe button: while the press lasts, its gold deepens to the dark bronze its :active rule names; let go and the resting gold returns (letting go on the button also submits the form, as a submit does — this page simply loads again).

2 — a link's :focus dress

Press and hold this link to the finishing test without letting go. The press gives the link the focus, as a press does in a real browser, and while the focus rests on it the link wears its :focus dress: deep red letters on pale gold, in place of its resting blue. Let go outside the link and the dress stays — the focus is still resting there; press anywhere else on the page, or click into the form above, and the focus moves on and the dress lifts. Let go on the link itself and it follows its link, as links do.

3 — a link's :active dress

Press and hold this second link. While the press lasts it wears its :active dress — white letters on dark red. The dress is the press's own: the moment you let go, on or off the link, it lifts.

4 — a bar that sticks to the top

Scroll slowly through the green section below. Its crimson bar declares position: sticky with top: 0. Its seat is reserved in the flow — the words never jump — and once scrolling would carry the bar above the top of the window, it holds there instead, riding over the section's paragraphs as they pass beneath it. When the section's own bottom edge reaches it, the bar is pushed away with its section: it may never leave the green.

6 — what is not on this page

Transforms, transitions and keyframes, column-span, and JavaScript remain unbuilt — the engine's own header says so, and this page promises none of them. Selects and textareas are live since 0.31, as the notes at the top say. Everything above this section is the whole of the interaction set, stated only as it was built.
